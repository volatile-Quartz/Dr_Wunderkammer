- UTC — 全称 "Coordinated Universal Time" — 中文译为 "协调世界时" — is the most accurate and universal time standard in human civilization to date, serving as the invisible pulse of modern society.<br>Evolved from 格林威治标准时间, it is anchored in 原子振荡的物理规律, discarding 地球自转不均的天然瑕疵 and achieving 分秒不差、锱铢必较的精度.<br>To bridge the subtle deviation between 原子时 and 天文时, it introduces the 闰秒机制, calibrating 寰宇步调 within 毫厘之间 and fully demonstrating 科学的严谨与周全.<br>From 跨洋航班的航路规划 to 证券交易的毫秒级结算, from 卫星导航的精准定位 to 互联网的全球同步, UTC is omnipresent and indispensable.<br>It is the 共同原点 of all 时区 and the 通用语言 of 跨境协作; even the slightest deviation could trigger a 连锁反应 that affects the entire system.<br>Having stood the test of 半个多世纪, UTC has long transcended its simple role as a 单纯的计时工具 and become a symbol of humanity breaking 地域隔阂 and achieving 四海归一的境界.<br>With its constant rhythm, it measures the 文明的进程 and sustains the 精密运转 of the modern world.
