## 2026.5.21.木（小满）
- 小满, the eighth of the 二十四节气 and the second solar term of summer, falls between May 20 and 22 on the 公历. At this time, nature strikes a delicate balance — a state of approaching fullness without excess. In the north, wheat kernels enter the milk-ripe stage, swollen yet unripe, giving rise to the saying "小满小满，麦粒渐满". In the south, rivers swell with seasonal rains but stop short of overflowing, as captured in the saying "小满小满，江河渐满".<br>The name 小满 itself is a masterstroke of observation and philosophy. It does not denote literal incompleteness but celebrates a state of nearing fulfillment without tipping into excess — a reflection of the ancient wisdom "月满则亏，水满则溢". True abundance, as Chinese tradition teaches, lies not in reaching the peak but in preserving room for growth.<br>This ethos permeates 小满's 三候: 一候苦菜秀 — bitter greens flourish, cleansing internal heat; 二候靡草死 — shade-tender grasses wither under the intensifying yang energy; 三候麦秋至 — wheat enters "grain autumn", a term that hints at harvest even as summer holds sway. Notably, the 二十四节气 do not include a "大满". This absence is deliberate — a cultural safeguard against the principle of 盈满则亏. As 曾国藩 upheld "花未全开月未圆" as life's ideal, so too did 欧阳修 write in his five-character quatrain: "最爱垄头麦，迎风笑落红". Both honor 小满's essence: ripeness deferred, potential preserved.<br>In agriculture, 小满's dual nature shapes regional practices.
